# Product user flows

Phase 1 plans these primary flows:

1. Register, verify email, sign in, and recover an account through Keycloak.
2. Provision Wi-Fi over BLE, claim a device, and associate it with a tank/organization.
3. Create or clone a user-defined algae profile, publish a version, and assign it.
4. View current and historical mock readings with timestamp quality and profile version.
5. Receive, acknowledge, and resolve alerts.
6. Invite members, change roles, revoke access, and transfer ownership.
7. Send permitted device/configuration commands and inspect delivery status.
8. Schedule a signed OTA release and observe success or rollback when authorized.

See [onboarding](device-onboarding.md), [sharing](device-sharing.md), [profile flow](algae-profile-flow.md), and [roles](role-permissions.md).
